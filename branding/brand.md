Brand guide — Poisha

Visual direction: "Royal & Wealthy" primary, with youthful mascot as optional variant.

Primary palette (Royal):
- Royal Blue: #1C3D7A
- Royal Gold: #D4AF37
- Charcoal: #0F1724
- Cream: #FFF8E7

Secondary palette (Prosperity / accent):
- Deep Green: #0B6E4F
- Teal (youth accent): #0FA3A3

Typography:
- Bangla: Noto Sans Bengali or SolaimanLipi
- Headings (Latin): Poppins / Montserrat
- Body (Latin): Inter / Noto Sans

Logo variants included in this branch:
- logo-royal.svg — emblem/coin + crown
- logo-mascot.svg — friendly mascot option (Bangla wordmark)

Microcopy (Bangla-first examples)
- Landing headline: "পয়শা — আপনার ডিজিটাল ধন"
- CTA primary: "এখন শুরু করুন"
- Onboarding short: "এক মিনিটেই শুরু করুন — ফোন দিতে হবে, ওটিপি দিয়ে ভেরিফাই করুন"
- Referral CTA: "বন্ধুকে শেয়ার করুন — দুজনেই 100 PISHA পান"

Usage notes
- Use the emblem (logo-royal.svg) as the primary app icon and header logo.
- Use mascot in social posts and campaigns targeting youth and memes.
- Keep gold accent for CTAs and highlights; keep backgrounds dark navy or cream for trust pages.
